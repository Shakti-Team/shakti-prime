SHAKTI ONE — Account & Data Deletion

Google Play Data Safety Compliant

At SHAKTI ONE, we respect your privacy and provide you with complete control over your account and personal data. As per Google Play's User Data & Data Safety Policy, this page explains how you can permanently delete your SHAKTI ONE account and associated personal telemetry records.

Option 1: In-App Account Deletion (Instant)

📱 How to delete your account directly inside the SHAKTI ONE App:

  1. Open the SHAKTI ONE Mobile App on your Android device.
  2. Tap on your Profile / Settings icon in the navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account & Security section.
  4. Tap on "Delete Account & Wipe Data".
  5. Confirm your decision. Your account, authentication credentials, emergency contacts, and journey history will be wiped immediately.

Option 2: Web / Email Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

✉️ Request Permanent Account & Data Deletion via Email:

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can submit a manual deletion request to our Data Protection Officer:

  • Send an email to: TeamSamayshakti.ai.in@gmail.com
  • Subject: Account and Data Deletion Request - Shakti App
  • Body Details: Mention your registered phone number or Google Sign-In email associated with your Shakti account.
Submit Deletion Request Email

Processing Time: Manual deletion requests are validated and permanently purged within 48 to 72 hours.

What Data Is Deleted Upon Request

  • Personal Profile: Full name, profile photo, registered email, and mobile phone number.
  • Emergency Contacts: All saved guardian phone numbers and pairing tokens.
  • GPS Location Telemetry: All historical GPS breadcrumbs, Safe Walk journey logs, and emergency SOS event markers.
  • Authentication Tokens: All active sessions, Firebase/Google authentication tokens, and on-device secure keys.

🔒 Data Retention & Purge Commitment

SHAKTI ONE does not retain your location telemetry, personal contacts, or audio files after your account is deleted. All server records and associated backups are irreversibly removed from our systems.
