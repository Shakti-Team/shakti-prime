🔒 Our Immutable Zero-Data-Brokering Pledge

Shakti is an autonomous women safety and life-protection platform. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal information, live location telemetry, emergency contacts, or audio data to any third-party advertisers or commercial entities. Your data is used exclusively to protect your personal safety during transit and emergency distress events.

1. Introduction & Purpose

This Privacy Policy explains how Shakti Safety Team ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, encrypts, and handles your information when you use the Shakti Mobile Application ("App") and our official website (https://shaktisafety.in).

2. Permissions & Data Collection Disclosures

To deliver real-time life safety, distress detection, and autonomous emergency dispatch, Shakti requests the following device permissions:

  • Location (Fine, Coarse & Background Location):
    Purpose: Enables real-time GPS telemetry sharing with your designated emergency guardians and emergency helplines when you activate an SOS alert, trigger a Safe Walk journey, or voice distress anomaly is detected. Background location operates continuously during active emergency events even when the app is minimized or the device screen is locked.
  • Microphone (VoiceGuard Distress Detection):
    Purpose: Used for real-time on-device acoustic keyword/scream analysis (VoiceGuard anomaly trigger) and sending voice clips to guardians during an active SOS. Audio streams are processed in transient device memory and are not stored permanently on remote servers for advertising.
  • Emergency Contacts (Read Contacts):
    Purpose: Allows you to select trusted guardian phone numbers directly from your address book who will receive instant SOS alerts, live tracking links, and emergency SMS broadcasts.
  • SMS Permission (SEND_SMS):
    Purpose: Allows the application to dispatch urgent SOS alert text messages with GPS coordinates to your chosen guardians in scenarios where cellular internet/mobile data is unavailable.
  • Foreground Service & Notifications:
    Purpose: Keeps the life-safety listener and live guardian telemetry service active in the background, displaying a persistent notification to ensure the user is always aware when safety monitoring is active.

3. How Information Is Shared

Your emergency telemetry and location data are shared strictly under the following conditions:

  • With Your Designated Guardians: Live GPS coordinates and alert alerts are broadcast only to contacts specifically selected and authenticated by you.
  • Emergency Services & Helplines: When SOS dispatch is triggered, coordinates may be routed to emergency response personnel or authorities at your request.
  • Legal & Life Safety Compliance: When required by applicable law, court order, or to prevent imminent physical harm to any individual.

4. Data Security & Encryption Standards

We implement enterprise-grade security protocols to protect your personal data:

  • All data transmissions between your device and our telemetry infrastructure are secured using TLS/SSL 256-bit encryption.
  • Sensitive user credentials and tokens are stored securely in encrypted on-device keychains (Android KeyStore / Flutter Secure Storage).

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full ownership and control over your personal data:

  • You can remove or edit emergency guardians, profile information, and permission settings at any time within the app.
  • To request permanent deletion of your account and all associated records from our servers, you can use the in-app "Delete Account" button or email us directly at TeamSamayshakti.ai.in@gmail.com. All telemetry records are permanently purged within 30 days of request.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Standards)

Shakti is intended for general audiences aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under 13 years of age without verified parental consent.
